How the verdict is decided

Grey market premium
  • 30% or more: +3
  • 15–30%: +2
  • 5–15%: +1
  • Below 0%: −2
Subscription (latest day)
  • QIB 10x+: +2, QIB 2x+: +1
  • Total 20x+: +2, 5x+: +1
  • Under 1x on the last day: −1
  • QIBs usually bid on the last day, so early days count less
Business & price
  • Revenue and profit growing: +1
  • Reasonable P/E (under ~25): +1; very rich (over 40): −1
  • Loss-making: −2
  • Mostly offer-for-sale or heavy debt: −1
Verdict
  • Apply: 5 or more
  • Listing gains only: 3–4, sell on listing day
  • Wait: 1–2, decide after final-day QIB numbers
  • Avoid: 0 or less
  • SME issues need a higher bar and are marked high-risk

GMP is an unofficial, unregulated grey-market quote. It changes quickly, differs between sources, and is not a guarantee of the listing price. Stock signals are a simple checklist (trend averages, profit growth, valuation, analyst targets). This page is research to help you decide; it is not investment advice, and the verdicts are produced by a fixed checklist, not by a SEBI-registered adviser.
